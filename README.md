**MotaCoin** 2.0.1 has been released! This upgrade brings lots of goodies to the Mota community, which include an all new Bridge tab in all our of Desktop wallets (Windows, Mac, Linux, Pi). This bridge allows for users to bridge their MotaCoin on/off Solana effortlessly using our new onchain bridge protocol. After five block confirmations, enjoy all the perks of being a solana token. Whether that's trading on the DEX directly from your wallet, and integrating it into your latest smart contract or bot, you also have the option to send your Mota back onto MotaCoin mainchain and start collecting some stake rewards! We have introduced a 4.20% APR for mainchain staking rewards, with a new max supply of 125m MotaCoin. The 2.0 Upgrade also brings a fun new feature called Stake for Charity (Aka Multisend) which allows you to divert variable percentages of your staking rewards to up to five different address per wallet. The transaction fee has also been increased to 0.0058 MOTA per TX.
